# Research Thinking Notes: New Zealand Blood Service

## Sources Processed

- Local website files from `nzbloodwebsite/` folder (49 HTML pages)
- Content extracted using Python HTML parsing
- No external web crawling of nzblood.co.nz per instructions

## Key Findings Summary

### Organization Structure
- Crown Entity established 1998
- Governed under Human Tissue Act 2008, Crown Entities Act 2004
- Minister of Health responsible (Hon Simeon Brown)
- Public Benefit Entity classification
- Replaced multiple regional blood services previously based in hospitals

### Operational Scale
- Requires 5,000+ donations per week nationally
- Less than 4% of eligible NZ population are blood donors
- One donation can save up to 3 lives
- Blood donation produces 13+ products

### Physical Infrastructure
- 11 permanent donor centres identified:
  - North Island: Constellation Drive, North Shore, Epsom, Manukau (Auckland area), Hamilton, Tauranga, Palmerston North, Wellington
  - South Island: Christchurch, Dunedin, Tuam St (Christchurch)
- Mobile blood drives supplement fixed locations
- All centres have recovery lounges, parking facilities

### Donation Types Documented
1. Whole blood (allogeneic) - 470ml per donation
2. Plasma (apheresis) - up to 60 minutes
3. Platelets - requires special assessment
4. Bone marrow - via NZ Bone Marrow Donor Registry
5. Hip bone - during hip replacement surgery
6. Tissue - post-mortem (eye tissue, heart valves, skin)
7. Organs - partnered with Organ Donation NZ

### Blood Products (Fresh)
- Red Blood Cells: 35-day shelf life
- Platelets: 7-day shelf life
- Fresh Frozen Plasma: 2-year shelf life

### Plasma-Derived Products
- Albumex 20 / Alburex NZ 20 & 5
- Biostate (Factor VIII) - haemophilia treatment
- Cryoprecipitate
- Rh(D) Immunoglobin-VF (Anti-D)
- Prothrombinex-VF (Factors II, IX, X)
- Normal Immunoglobulin
- Plus various immunoglobulins (Privigen, Gamunex, Hizentra)

### Eligibility Framework
- Age: 16-71 for new donors
- Existing donors can continue to 75, potentially 81 with approval
- Minimum weight: 50kg
- Height/weight matrix for first-time donors
- Comprehensive A-Z medical conditions list
- Travel restrictions for malaria/other endemic areas
- Behavioral criteria recently updated (MSM deferral reduced to 3 months Dec 2020)
- vCJD (mad cow) restriction lifted Feb 2024 for UK/France/Ireland residents

### Recent Policy Changes Noted
- Feb 2024: vCJD restriction removed
- Nov 2021: Multiple criteria relaxed (age, transfusion wait times, tattoo wait reduced to 3 months)
- Dec 2020: MSM deferral reduced from 12 to 3 months
- 2025: Medsafe approved individualized donor assessment (IDA)
- Testing upgraded to 5-NAT (adds Parvovirus B19 and Hepatitis A)
- IDA expected implementation in 2026

### Healthcare Professional Services

- Haemovigilance programme and annual reporting
- Reference Laboratory services
- NZ Transplantation and Immunogenetics Laboratory (NZTIL)
- Blood Banks network
- Clinical guidelines and policies
