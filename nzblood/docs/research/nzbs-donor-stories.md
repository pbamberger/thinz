# NZBS Donor Stories - Real Impact Examples

These stories from the NZBS website illustrate the real-world impact of blood donation.

---

## Michelle's Story - 80 Donations Saved Her Life

Michelle was diagnosed with Stage 4 Hodgkin Lymphoma in August 2011 after suffering from fatigue and breathing difficulties for six months. With two daughters aged one and three at the time, she had pushed through thinking symptoms would pass.

A 15cm tumour was discovered in her chest. She underwent chemotherapy immediately.

Her journey included:
- Cardiac arrest and resuscitation
- A stroke that caused her to lose her speech
- Losing friends to cancer

**It took 80 blood donations to save Michelle's life.**

"It makes me want to cry," she says. "I wouldn't be here without those blood donors. There are no words to express my gratitude."

Now in remission, Michelle promotes blood donation through her Facebook page and has encouraged colleagues to donate 77 units of blood. She notes that she ironically never gave blood before getting ill due to a needle phobia.

"If you've got healthy veins, it's a very small price to pay to save someone's life."

## Brooke and Eli's Story - 15 Transfusions for a Premature Baby

In March 2009, Brooke Hopkins went into labour early. Due to an existing health condition, she required emergency caesarean surgery. Brooke herself nearly needed a blood transfusion.

Baby Eli was born nearly **16 weeks premature at just 638 grams**.

He spent his first four months of life in hospital, fighting lung and tummy infections. **15 blood transfusions kept Eli alive.**

"Blood donors could have saved my life, but they certainly saved Eli's," says Brooke.

Now a healthy child, Eli is described as "a little rocket" - affectionate, loves people and animals, and naturally giving. He once brought flowers, a toy, and a handmade card to a sick neighbour.

"The truth is that some people wouldn't survive without a blood transfusion. Giving blood is a relatively easy and simple way to save a life, or many lives."

## Jade's Story - Inspired at School

Jade was inspired to donate at age 14-15 after an NZBS school presentation. Not afraid of needles or blood, she began donating as soon as eligible and as often as possible.

Over the years, she had to pause donations for:
- Tongue piercing
- Tattoo
- Four pregnancies and breastfeeding

"I imagine that if me, my partner, my children, friends and family are ever in the situation of needing blood, that it's there."

Jade values receiving messages when her blood is used: "It's humbling to know I've helped save a life."

## Darren's Story - 215 Plasma Donations

Darren from Palmerston North is one of NZBS's most frequent plasma donors, having completed **215 plasma donations**.

Beyond donating, Darren participates in community events. In 2019, he joined 45 fellow cyclists in the BDO Lake Taupo challenge, raising over $12,000 for HeartKids NZ.

---

## Bill's Story - Donating Since 1973

Bill has been giving blood since 1973 when he was 19 years old. As an active sportsperson, he was aware of the demand for blood products due to sports and accident injuries.

"It seemed a natural and easy thing to do for most healthy people."

He notes that technology has improved dramatically over decades: "The discomfort/pricking is almost nil these days... it is a painless experience in my view."

"I would encourage all healthy people to donate blood as we never know when we or our families/friends may also need to receive it at some stage."

## Key Takeaways from These Stories

| Story | Donations Required | Context |
|-------|-------------------|---------|
| Michelle | 80 donations | Stage 4 lymphoma treatment |
| Baby Eli | 15 transfusions | Premature birth at 638g |
| Darren | 215 donations given | Long-term plasma donor |
| Bill | Donating since 1973 | 50+ years of regular donation |

These stories demonstrate that:
- Cancer patients may require dozens of donations during treatment
- Premature infants often need multiple transfusions to survive
- Regular donors can make hundreds of contributions over a lifetime
- Blood donation truly is a community effort saving lives across generations
