## Quick Reference

### What NZBS Does
- Collects, processes, and supplies blood, plasma, and blood products
- Operates 11 donor centres across New Zealand
- Manages NZ Bone Marrow Donor Registry
- Provides clinical services to healthcare professionals
- Coordinates tissue and organ donation programs

### Key Statistics
- **5,000+ donations needed weekly**
- **<4% of eligible population donates**
- **1 donation can save up to 3 lives**
- **13+ products made from blood donations**
